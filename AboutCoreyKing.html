About Corey King

Corey King holds a Bachelor of Science degree in economics from the University of Florida in Gainesville and a Juris Doctorate from American University in Washington, DC. 

Corey King has practiced law in Florida since 1998. Corey has extensive experience in court, having tried over 100 cases and assisted judges for 15 years.

Corey King has worked as a staff attorney in Florida's 8th Judicial Circuit in Gainesville, Florida's 17th Judicial Circuit in Fort Lauderdale, and Florida's 2nd District Court of Appeals in Lakeland; as an Assistant Public Defender in Tampa; and as a lawyer in private practice in Gainesville and Fort Lauderdale.
